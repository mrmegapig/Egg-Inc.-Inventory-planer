No-Cap Piggy Guide

Preparation

Determine which artifacts you won’t need for crafting. Save ALL components needed, sometimes down to the T1 artifacts required for crafting all the way up the chain. Regularly consume unneeded artifacts (Fridays are good). Avoid consuming during the event due to lag.

During the Event

DON'T CONSUME: Gold (meteorites), Geodes, Titanium, Prophecy stones & fragments, Clarity stones & fragments. Be judicious with Soul stones & fragments (needed for Prophecy stones).

CONSUME:

  • All fragments not listed in the "Don't consume" list.
  • Stray artifacts with small stacks.
  • Then consume the newly created fragments.
